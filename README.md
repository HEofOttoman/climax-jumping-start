# climax-jumping-start
Haven onboarding jumpstart game. Jumping! Palantir!

Simple platformer where you play as Saruman the White and have to find your misplaced Palantir. You will then use it to spy on Australians, finding quite an *interesting* sight. 

### How to Play
Go to [https://henry-wauzivuff.itch.io/pltr](https://henry-wauzivuff.itch.io/climax-jumping). Use WASD/spacebar or ←↑→ to control. If you ever get stuck, there's a pause menu to restart.
